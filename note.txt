## **Question 2: What challenges did you face and how did you develop?**

\- **Understanding Existing Application Architecture, Selectron S3 Database Tables and Partner APIs:** Understanding the existing application architecture and integration patterns required exploring Selectron S3 database tables, partner API endpoints, and the underlying reward issuance workflows. Invested time in understanding how partner APIs expose and exchange data, how reward issuance is processed, and how different application components interact. This helped strengthen my understanding of the existing ecosystem and design solutions that align with established architectural patterns and integration requirements.

\- **Cross-Team Collaboration Across Time Zones:** Coordinating with predominantly onshore UI, Athena, and business teams while working with limited overlapping hours required effective communication and planning. Addressed this by sharing discussion notes, documenting open questions, and scheduling important meetings during overlapping hours to clarify requirements and resolve dependencies. This helped maintain alignment and manage delivery timelines effectively.

\- **Business and Integration Requirement Clarifications:** Certain implementation aspects required additional clarification around Rewards business flows, client data exchange, API integrations, and data security expectations. Proactively raised questions with business and dependent teams, discussed different scenarios, and worked towards obtaining clarity before proceeding with implementation. This helped reduce ambiguity and ensure solutions addressed the expected business and technical requirements.

\- **Maintaining Code Quality Alongside Faster Development:** With development progressing across multiple team members, manually reviewing code changes and ensuring consistency with existing architectural patterns became challenging. Addressed this through regular demos, code walkthrough sessions, and collaborative reviews. These sessions helped improve understanding of implementations, identify potential issues, maintain clean coding practices, and ensure alignment with established architecture.

\- **Strengthening Technical Decision-Making Through Exploration:** Working on newer solution areas, particularly the Rewards 360 Degree Chatbot, involved exploring unfamiliar concepts and evaluating different technical approaches. Used hands-on experimentation, technical research, and AI-assisted exploration to understand the possibilities, validate design decisions, and develop more effective implementation approaches. This strengthened my ability to independently explore solutions and translate ideas into practical technical designs.
