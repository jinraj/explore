# 1. Reflecting on the goals you had for this year, what were your key accomplishments?

### A. Rewards Project – Technical Ownership and Solution Engineering

- **Requirement Analysis and Solution Exploration:** Took an active role in understanding business requirements, identifying different use cases, challenging existing assumptions, and raising relevant questions to uncover functional gaps and edge cases before implementation. Evaluated alternative approaches and actively contributed to solution discussions to arrive at effective technical decisions.

- **Schema Design and Technical Improvements:** Contributed to designing and refining data schemas, supporting infrastructure, and validation processes, focusing on data consistency, integrity, and maintainability. Explored improvements to existing structures to accommodate evolving business requirements and ensure better implementation efficiency.

- **Technical Collaboration and Delivery:** Actively participated in technical discussions and collaborated with UI and other dependent teams to resolve technical dependencies. Focused on establishing consistent implementation approaches, sharing knowledge, and improving team efficiency through open discussions and collaborative problem-solving.

- **Team Guidance and Knowledge Sharing:** Guided junior engineers in understanding the project scope, application architecture, development workflows, and implementation approaches. Provided technical guidance, helped resolve development challenges, and supported their understanding of business requirements and overall project functionality.

### B. Rewards 360 Degree Chatbot – Solution Conceptualisation, Architecture and Innovation

- **Solution Conceptualisation and Architecture:** Took the initiative in conceptualising the Rewards 360 Degree Chatbot and building its overall architecture and technical direction. Independently explored how an AI-powered chatbot could integrate with the existing Rewards application and its internal services to provide an intelligent, dynamic, and conversational experience.

- **Intelligent Query Handling and Token Optimisation:** Designed an approach to minimise unnecessary LLM consumption by separating predefined questions and responses from queries requiring AI-generated responses. Explored segregation of data sources, database information, Q&A content, and knowledge-base information to optimise query handling and reserve expensive LLM calls for scenarios where intelligent response generation was actually required.

- **Dynamic GraphQL Integration and Prompt Engineering:** Designed the chatbot's interaction approach to dynamically interpret user queries, identify the relevant data requirements, and generate appropriate GraphQL queries based on available API capabilities. Focused on prompt engineering to ensure the AI could interpret API JSON responses, understand the returned data, and generate accurate, contextual, and natural English responses without relying on rigid, predefined response structures.

- **Conversational Experience and Rapid Implementation:** Incorporated voice-based response functionality, allowing users to listen to chatbot responses through an interactive voice option. Conceptualisation and architectural exploration were completed within a day, followed by implementation on the next day, leveraging AI-assisted development capabilities to accelerate the overall development process.

- **AI Hackathon Achievement:** The Rewards 360 Degree Chatbot solution contributed to securing **first place in the AI Hackathon conducted by the OCPM team**. The solution demonstrated the potential of combining intelligent query handling, optimised LLM usage, dynamic data retrieval, and natural-language response generation to deliver an effective conversational experience.

### C. Artificial Intelligence Enablement – Responsible AI Adoption

- **AI-Assisted Development with IntelliJ Copilot:** Regularly leveraged IntelliJ Copilot for code implementation, understanding existing project structures, analysing implementation patterns followed by other team members, and reviewing code changes and merges. This helped accelerate development and improve familiarity with different areas of the codebase.

- **Optum Copilot for Technical Problem-Solving:** Used Optum Copilot for project-specific architectural questions, exploring design alternatives, evaluating implementation approaches, and identifying possible solutions to technical challenges. Leveraged it to accelerate technical research and validate different approaches.

- **ChatGPT for Technical Learning and Exploration:** Used ChatGPT to deepen understanding of software architecture, coding practices, design patterns, and emerging concepts, particularly around chatbot architecture, LLM integration, prompt engineering, token optimisation, and integrating AI capabilities into existing enterprise applications.

- **Responsible and Effective AI Adoption:** Followed a validation-driven approach by reviewing, testing, and adapting AI-generated code and recommendations before applying them. Used AI as a productivity and engineering problem-solving partner while retaining ownership of technical decisions, implementation quality, and security considerations.
